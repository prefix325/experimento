\section{Method}
\ResearchMethodEN

The preliminary design contains three conditions: a DPCA numerical reference; a local LLM receiving a structured temporal representation without DPCA output; and a local LLM assisted by DPCA evidence, pending a scope decision. The LLM output is constrained to normal, anomaly, or insufficient evidence, followed by a traceable justification. Alarm persistence, abstention, prompt version, random seeds where applicable, and all execution parameters must be frozen before the formal experiment.

\section{Discussion}
\ResearchLimitationsEN

The intended analysis will distinguish numerical detection ability from explanatory usefulness. DPCA is not framed as an adversary that the LLM must defeat; it provides an objective statistical reference and contributing-variable evidence. Any claim of industrial relevance will remain bounded by the public and simulated nature of the TEP environment.
