# Manuscript integrity policy

A manuscript may advance only when every in-text citation resolves to a verified source and every bibliographic source is cited in the text. Each substantive claim must have a claim record and, when evidence is required, one or more reviewed evidence bindings with source, locator, support type and scope assessment.

Sources progress through identity, access and evidence gates. `CITABLE` requires verified metadata and legitimate full-text access. Changes to source identity, version or claim wording invalidate dependent bindings until re-review. Bibliographies are generated from actual citations; orphan references and dangling citations are blocking errors.

Direct quotations require exact text and locator verification. Secondary citations must be disclosed. Counterevidence and contextual disagreement are mandatory for central propositions. Source relevance alone is insufficient: BANCA must confirm that the evidence supports, limits or contradicts the exact claim.

Similarity control combines local source fingerprints, exact n-gram checks, external exact-phrase searches and, for a final institutional submission, a commercial closed-corpus report when available. Similarity is reviewed, not interpreted as plagiarism automatically. Unresolved findings make the manuscript `NOT_SUBMITTABLE`.

The integrity score is computed from source identity, citation round-trip, claim-evidence coverage, fidelity, quotation controls, counterevidence, similarity review, AI disclosure, cross-document consistency and human authority. A web-only similarity review is capped at 990/1000; 1000 requires a reviewed commercial-engine report.
